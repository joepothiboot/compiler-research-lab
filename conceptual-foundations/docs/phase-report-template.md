# Phase report template

Copy the skeleton to `reports/phase-N.md` when a phase ends. Keep every
heading, and write "none" rather than deleting a section. The roadmap gives the
phase's objective, checkpoint and stop conditions.

## Skeleton

```markdown
# Phase N: <title>

## Objective

<Copied from the roadmap.>

## Study completed

<Each section read, by resource and heading, and whether it was finished,
stopped by its stop condition, or skipped as optional.>

## Experiments

<One line per experiment: ID, question, outcome in one sentence, and where its
record is. Records follow docs/experiment-template.md.>

## Outputs

<Files created or changed, with their paths.>

## Findings

<Each finding labeled F (with its evidence), H, A or U. A finding that claims a
bug, a gap or a problem also carries a label from the classification table in
docs/experiment-template.md.>

## Failed assumptions

<What I believed at the start of the phase that turned out wrong, and what
showed it.>

## Open questions

## Checkpoint

<The roadmap's checkpoint for this phase: the decision (continue, skim or
defer) and the evidence for it.>

## Next steps
```
